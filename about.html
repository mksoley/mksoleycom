Founder & Lead Psychiatrist

Dr. Mustafa K. Soley, MD

Dr. Soley founded Soley Psychiatry in 1994 with a clear mission: to provide compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care to the San Francisco community. Over three decades, he has built a team of highly skilled specialists covering every aspect of mental health.

Fluent in both English and Turkish, Dr. Soley is especially committed to serving multicultural patients who benefit from care provided in their native language. He practices during the first week of each month.
